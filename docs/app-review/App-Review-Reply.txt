2. Purpose and target audience
WordAhead helps English learners, particularly users who prefer Korean guidance, understand English transcripts and build a personal vocabulary list. Learners can check word meanings and pronunciation, save words in context, and review unfamiliar vocabulary. Recommendations use local learning history and rules; they do not use an external AI service or claim to measure proficiency.

3. Access and main features
No login credentials or setup files are required.
Launch the app and open one of the included practice transcripts. Tap an English word to see its definition and pronunciation. Tap the speaker button to hear it, and tap + to save it. Mark familiar words using the checkbox. Open “내 단어장” to review/filter/delete saved words. Use “먼저 살펴볼 단어” to preview unfamiliar vocabulary.
Use “자막 직접 붙여넣기” to enter a transcript that you own or have permission to use, or select “예시 자막 불러오기” for the included sample. Familiar-word backups can be exported/imported as JSON; these are not full transcript/vocabulary backups. “학습 데이터 삭제” clears local learning records after confirmation.
YouTube links can request available English captions. Private, restricted, unavailable or captionless videos may fail; the error screen offers manual transcript input. The official YouTube IFrame Player is embedded in the reader; tapping a transcript time seeks the player. Playback pauses outside the reader or when the app enters the background. A temporary inactive state, such as opening Control Center, does not trigger an app-issued pause. Restricted embeds offer a YouTube link. Videos are not downloaded and playlists are not retrieved.

5. Regional behavior
The app does not intentionally enable different core learning features by region. The interface primarily uses Korean with English learning content. External YouTube/Wiktionary access, available captions, and system English voices can vary by region, network and device settings. Included transcripts, local vocabulary storage and the bundled dictionary provide the core learning path without requiring successful caption imports.
